    Car service in Sardulgarh and Mohali.

    Saggu Motor Garage has been working on cars in Sardulgarh since 1997, under the promise on our badge: quality service.

    We take five cars a day at each of our two branches, in Sardulgarh and Mohali. Each car has its own bay and its own drop-off time, which you choose when you book.
