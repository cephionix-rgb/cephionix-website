How bookingworks here.

The rules for booking a bay at Saggu Motor Garage, in plain words. Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version

  • One booking is one bay, at one branch, on one day.
  • Each branch takes five cars a day.
  • You get a booking token and an email confirmation.
  • Cancel any time with your mobile number and token.
  • The work and its cost are agreed with you at the garage.

Who we are

These terms cover the booking service on this website, run by Saggu Motor Garage. We have been working on cars in Sardulgarh since 1997 and now have two branches, in Sardulgarh and in Mohali.

By making a booking you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not book through the website. You can still contact the garage directly.

Booking a bay

  • A booking holds one bay at one branch on one day, with the drop-off time shown for that bay.
  • Each branch takes at most five cars a day. When all five bays are booked, the day shows as full.
  • You can book up to 30 days ahead. Days marked closed on the calendar cannot be booked.
  • One mobile number can hold one booking per day, across both branches.
  • Your booking is confirmed when your bay and booking token appear on the screen.

Your details and your email

To book, we ask for your name, mobile number, email address, car make and model, and the service you need. You can add a note about the car.

We use these details to run your booking: to hold your bay, to send you a confirmation email and a cancellation email if you cancel, and to contact you about your visit. Garage staff at the branch you chose can see them.

Please give an email address that you can open. Check your spam folder if the confirmation does not arrive. The email is a copy of your booking; the bay is held for you even if the email is delayed.

Your booking token

When you book, you receive a short booking token. With your mobile number, it lets you find or cancel the booking from the website.

Keep the token private. Anyone who has both your mobile number and your token can cancel your booking.

Changing or cancelling

  • Open Already booked? in the booking section, enter your mobile number and token, and cancel from there. The bay becomes free for other customers straight away.
  • To change a booking, cancel it and book a new bay. A cancellation email is sent to the address on the booking.
  • If you cannot use the website, contact the branch you booked.

Your visit

Please arrive at the drop-off time on your ticket and tell us what you have noticed with the car. If you will be late, contact the branch as early as you can.

What the car needs, what it will cost and how long it will take are discussed and agreed with you at the garage. This website does not take payments and does not give quotes.

Closed days and changes by us

The garage may close a day or a branch, for example for a holiday or a day without staff. A closed day cannot be booked. If we have to change a booking that already exists, we will contact you on the mobile number or email you gave.

Using the website fairly

Book only for a real car that you plan to bring. To keep bays available for everyone, the website limits how many requests one device can make, and bookings that look false or repeated may be cancelled.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The date at the top of the page shows when they last changed. A booking is covered by the terms that applied when you made it.

Questions about these terms or your booking: contact the branch, or message us on Instagram.
